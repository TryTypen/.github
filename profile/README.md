### Publishing, without the complexity.

Typen is an independent blogging platform built to make writing and publishing feel effortless.

Create your publication, make it your own, and focus on what matters: your words. We'll take care of the rest.

**Everything you need to make it yours.**

- **Your space, your style.** Beautiful themes, customizable typography, and your own blog address.
- **Just write.** A focused editor designed to keep the writing experience simple.
- **Built for the web.** Fast pages, SEO, RSS feeds, and Markdown support out of the box.
- **Grow your publication.** Collaborate with others, understand your readers, and connect your own domain.
- **A little extra magic.** Meet Cleo, an optional reading guide that brings helpful context to your stories.
